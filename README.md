# MLS-Travels-taxi-servies
MLSTravels is an end-to-end client product project for a taxi/cab business, covering requirement gathering, BRD/FRD, user workflows, MVP planning, product development, testing, and client delivery. Focus: Business Analysis, Product Management &amp; Project Management.
🚀 **From Business Requirement to a Working Product | Client Project**
https://mlstravel.lovable.app/

I’m happy to share a real-world project I recently worked on for a taxi/cab driver who manages multiple cars and coordinates with people interested in joining driving duties.

The challenge was to make the process of **sharing duties, collecting interested joiners, and managing information** more organized and easier for the owner.

### 🔍 My Role

I worked on the project from a **Business Analyst + Product Management + Project Management perspective**:

• Understood the client's business requirements
• Identified the key users and their needs
• Converted requirements into website features
• Designed the owner and joiner workflow
• Planned and prioritized the MVP features
• Built and tested the working website
• Iterated based on the client's requirements

### 🛠️ Tools & Approach:

**Lovable | Product Thinking | Requirement Gathering | User Flow | MVP | Testing | Project Planning**

The website allows the owner to manage information and post duty-related updates, while interested joiners can view the opportunities and respond through the defined workflow.

🔗 **Live Project:** https://mlstravel.lovable.app/
